Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to run your account and save your papers. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and your papers are visible only to you.

Paper Build (“we”, “us”) is a research paper editor available at kalpeshkatare.in/Paperbuild and as an Android app, run by Kalpesh Katare in India. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Paper Build, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name, and, if you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the basic profile those services share (name, email and profile picture). If you use email and password, your password is stored only in encrypted (hashed) form by our sign-in provider.
  • Your papers: when you are signed in, the papers you write (text, references, figures and tables) are saved to your account so you can open them on any device. Without an account, your paper stays only in your browser or app on your device.
  • Payments: if you buy Pro, payment is handled by Razorpay. We receive the order and payment IDs, amount, currency and plan. We never see or store your full card, UPI or bank details.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting providers keep standard logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) to keep the service secure and working.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores a few small settings on your device (theme, last paper, preferred currency) using your browser's local storage.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, keep your papers saved and give you the features of your plan.
  • To process Pro payments and keep payment records required by law.
  • To send account emails: confirmations, password resets and payment receipts. We don't send marketing emails without asking.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

Services we use

  • Supabase: stores accounts and saved papers.
  • Razorpay: processes payments.
  • Google and Microsoft: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • LanguageTool: only when you tap “Check my paper”, the text of your paper is sent to LanguageTool to check grammar and spelling. Clarity tips are worked out on your device.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy. We share only what each one needs to do its job.

Who can see your papers

Only you. Saved papers are protected so that each account can read and change only its own papers. We do not read, publish or share your papers, and we never use them to train AI models.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and papers until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit or delete any saved paper at any time in the editor.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and all your papers, by emailing us at the address below. We'll do it within 30 days.
  • You can use the editor without an account; then nothing is stored on our servers.

Children

Paper Build is meant for college students, researchers and other adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes we'll also let signed-in users know.
