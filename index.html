Questions

Is this accepted by journals?

Paper Build follows the general layout of each standard. Journals and conferences often add their own rules, such as page limits or figure formats, so always read their author guidelines before you submit.

Where is my paper saved?

On your device as you type, and in your Paper Build account when you're signed in, so you can open it on your phone or laptop. Free accounts keep 1 paper online; Pro keeps unlimited papers.

How does Pro billing work?

You pay once for a month (₹99) or a year (₹799) through Razorpay. It doesn't renew automatically; when it ends you go back to Free and keep your papers.

Can I switch standards after writing?

Yes. Your text stays the same; the layout, headings, citations and reference list are rebuilt for the new standard instantly.

Is the APK safe to install?

It's built automatically from this project's public source code on GitHub. Android asks for permission only because it doesn't come from the Play Store. The app asks for no special permissions.

Does it write the paper for me?

No. Paper Build formats your own writing. Colleges treat AI-written papers as plagiarism, so the writing stays yours.

Paper Build is not affiliated with IEEE, ACM, Springer, Elsevier, the APA, the MLA or the University of Chicago Press. Style names are used only to describe formats.
